# 📊 Excel Project 2 – Data Jobs Skills & Salary Analysis

## 🔎 Introduction  
This project is a continuation of my Excel learning journey, expanding on **Project 1 (Salary Dashboard)**.  
The focus here was to analyze the relationship between **skills and salaries** in the data job market, and explore how **skills, locations, and job types** influence pay.

---

## The dashboard allows users to:

Explore how different skills influence salaries and job opportunities

Identify high-paying and in-demand skills in the data industry

Gain insights into job distribution across roles and regions

---

## ⚒️ Excel Skills Used
- 📥 **Power Query** → Data cleaning & transformation  
- 🧩 **Power Pivot & Data Model** → Building table relationships  
- 🧮 **DAX Measures** → Median salary calculations  
- 📊 **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts** → Interactive analysis  
- 📈 **Advanced Visuals** → Scatter plots, combo charts, KPI cards  

---

## 📸 Dashboard Highlights

### 1️⃣ Median Salary Analysis  
- Used **DAX CALCULATE function** to analyze salary distribution.  
- Metrics derived:  
  - **Overall Median Salary**  
  - **US Median Salary**  
  - **Non-US Median Salary**  
- Insights are dynamic based on **Country slicer selection**.  
- Median values are compared across **Job Titles** for deeper understanding.

### 2️⃣ Skills & Salary Relationship  
- Scatter plot showing correlation between **number of skills** and **median salary**

### 3️⃣ Top 10 Skills by Salary & Likelihood

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- 🌍 US-based roles generally offer higher salaries, especially in technical roles.  
- 🛠️ Specialized skills (Python, SQL, Cloud tools) are tied to stronger pay growth.  
- 📈 Combining Power Query, Pivot Tables, and DAX makes Excel a **powerful analysis tool** comparable to BI platforms.
